# Data licence

`oliveira2020_data.csv` holds the 32 model indicators (n = 381) of:

Oliveira, T. (2020). *Data for: Why do people share their travel experiences on social media?* (Version 1) [Data set]. Mendeley Data. https://doi.org/10.17632/2g67vckxgz.1

The data are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 (CC BY-NC 3.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/. Attribution goes to the data set above and to:

Oliveira, T., Araujo, B., & Tam, C. (2020). Why do people share their travel experiences on social media? *Tourism Management, 78*, 104041. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tourman.2019.104041

This file is included only so that the analyses can be reproduced. The licence of the AI-SEM code does not cover it.
